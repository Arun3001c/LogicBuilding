If you want to round to the nearest multiple of k, divide by k, round, then multiply by k again.

In your problem, the nearest even number means the nearest multiple of 2.

Let's walk through your examples.

Original	Divide by 2	round()	Multiply by 2	Result
16.2	8.1	8	16	✅
16.8	8.4	8	16	✅
17.2	8.6	9	18	✅
17.8	8.9	9	18	✅

So instead of trying to decide whether to add 1 or subtract 1, you can directly round to the nearest even number.

Code for rounding to the nearest even number
day_time_hours = round((12 + (latitude / 90) * 12) / 2) * 2
Let's test it mentally

For latitude = 40:

Formula:
12 + (40 / 90) × 12
= 17.333...
Divide by 2:
8.666...
Round:
9
Multiply by 2:
18

So the rounded daytime hours are 18.

One more thing

This trick works for any multiple.

Nearest multiple of 5:
round(x / 5) * 5
Nearest multiple of 10:
round(x / 10) * 10
Nearest multiple of 100:
round(x / 100) * 100

It's a useful pattern to remember because it appears in many programming problems.
