In your specific example
int(8.319 * 100)

Step:

8.319 * 100 = 831.9
int(831.9) = 831

So here:

int(831.9) → 831
math.floor(831.9) → 831

👉 They give the same result in this case.

But the important difference
1. int() → truncates toward zero

It simply cuts off the decimal part.

Examples:

int(5.9)   → 5
int(-5.9)  → -5   # important
2. math.floor() → rounds down (toward −∞)

It always goes to the lower integer.

Examples:

math.floor(5.9)   → 5
math.floor(-5.9)  → -6   # different!
Why this matters

The difference only shows up with negative numbers.

Value	int()	floor()
5.9	5	5
-5.9	-5	-6
So which should you use?
Use int() when:
You just want to cut off decimals
You are working with positive numbers (like prices, percentages after scaling, etc.)
Use math.floor() when:
You need mathematically correct rounding down
You might deal with negative numbers
You want strict “always go down” behavior
For your case (8.319 * 100)

Both are fine, but:

int(8.319 * 100)

is simpler and more common in practice.
