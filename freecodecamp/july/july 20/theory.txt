max() is there to make your function work regardless of the order of the inputs.

Suppose the test is:

is_golden_ratio(21, 34)

There are two possible divisions:

21 / 34 = 0.6176
34 / 21 = 1.6190

The golden ratio is 1.618, so you want the larger of the two results.

Now suppose the test is reversed:

is_golden_ratio(34, 21)

The divisions become:

34 / 21 = 1.6190
21 / 34 = 0.6176

Again, you want 1.6190.

Using:

ratio = max(a / b, b / a)

means:

if a is larger, a / b is chosen.
if b is larger, b / a is chosen.

So the ratio is always the larger number divided by the smaller number.

An alternative that many programmers prefer is:

ratio = max(a, b) / min(a, b)

This makes your intention even clearer:

max(a, b) → larger number
min(a, b) → smaller number
divide larger by smaller

This is equivalent to using max(a / b, b / a), but it's easier to read because it directly says, "divide the larger number by the smaller one."

abs() stands for absolute value.

Its job is to remove the sign (+ or -) from a number.

Examples:

abs(5)     # 5
abs(-5)    # 5
abs(0.007) # 0.007
abs(-0.007)# 0.007
Why do we need it here?

The problem asks:

Is the ratio within 0.01 of 1.618?

The word within means the ratio can be:

a little larger than 1.618
a little smaller than 1.618
Example 1: Ratio is larger
ratio = 1.625
golden_ratio = 1.618

ratio - golden_ratio

Result:

0.007

This is within 0.01, so it should be True.

Example 2: Ratio is smaller
ratio = 1.611
golden_ratio = 1.618

ratio - golden_ratio

Result:

-0.007

The distance is still 0.007, so this should also be True.

But without abs(), you get -0.007.

With abs():

abs(-0.007)

Result:

0.007

Now both cases are treated the same.

Think of it as a number line
1.608      1.618      1.628
----|---------|---------|
      ^ target

A ratio of 1.611 is 0.007 away.

A ratio of 1.625 is also 0.007 away.

The distance is what matters, not whether the ratio is above or below the target.

That's exactly what abs() gives you.

So this line:

abs(ratio - 1.618) <= 0.01

reads as:

"Is the distance between ratio and 1.618 less than or equal to 0.01?"
