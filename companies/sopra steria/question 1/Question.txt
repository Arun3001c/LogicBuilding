Lucky Coupon Number

A supermarket generates coupon numbers for its customers. The marketing team believes that a coupon is luckier when the sum of its digits is higher.

You are given a coupon number N. Your task is to find a coupon number less than or equal to N whose digit sum is maximum. If multiple coupon numbers have the same maximum digit sum, return the largest coupon number among them.

Input Format:
A single integer N, representing the maximum coupon number allowed.

Output Format:
Print the coupon number less than or equal to N that has the maximum possible sum of digits.
If multiple numbers have the same maximum digit sum, print the largest number.

Constraints:
1 ≤ N ≤ 10^9


Example 1:

Input:
100

Output:
99

Explanation:

Number     Sum of Digits
100        1
99         18

Since 99 ≤ 100 and its digit sum (18) is maximum, the answer is 99.


Example 2:

Input:
500

Output:
499

Explanation:

Number     Sum of Digits
500        5
499        22

499 has the highest digit sum.


Example 3:

Input:
1234

Output:
999

Explanation:

Number     Sum of Digits
1234       10
999        27

999 is less than 1234 and has the maximum digit sum.
