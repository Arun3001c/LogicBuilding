Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock
Problem Statement
You are given an array prices where:

prices[i] = stock price on day i
You can buy on one day and sell on a later day.
Return the maximum profit you can achieve.
If no profit is possible, return 0.
Examples
Example 1:

Input:
prices = [7,1,5,3,6,4]

Output:
5



Explanation:

Buy on day 2:

price = 1



Sell on day 5:

price = 6



Profit:

6 - 1 = 5



Example 2:

Input:
prices = [7,6,4,3,1]

Output:
0



Because prices only decrease, so no profitable transaction exists.
