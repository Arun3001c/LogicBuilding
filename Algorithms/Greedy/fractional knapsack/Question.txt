Greedy Problem #6: Fractional Knapsack ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Problem Statement

A thief enters a jewelry store carrying a bag.

The bag can hold a maximum weight of W kilograms.

There are N items in the store.

Each item has:

Weight
Value

The thief wants to maximize the total value of the items placed in the bag.

Special Rule

Unlike the normal knapsack problem,

The thief is allowed to take a fraction of an item.

For example,

Suppose an item weighs 10 kg and is worth ₹100.

If only 4 kg of space is left,

the thief can take 4/10 of the item and receive

4/10 × 100 = ₹40
Input Format
N W
N → Number of items
W → Capacity of the bag

Next line:

Weight1 Weight2 Weight3 ... WeightN

Next line:

Value1 Value2 Value3 ... ValueN
Output Format

Print the maximum value that can be obtained.

Example 1
Input
3 50
10 20 30
60 100 120
Output
240
Explanation

Bag Capacity

50 kg

Items

Weight	Value
10	60
20	100
30	120

Take

Entire 10 kg item
Entire 20 kg item

Weight used

30 kg

Remaining

20 kg

Take

20/30

of the last item.

Value obtained

60 + 100 + 80 = 240
