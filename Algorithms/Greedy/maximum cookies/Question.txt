Problem: Assign Cookies

Imagine you have a group of children and a box of cookies.

Every child has a greed factor.

Every cookie has a size.

What does greed factor mean?

Suppose

Child A → Greed = 3

This means:

This child will only be happy if they get a cookie of size 3 or more.

So,

Cookie Size	Child Happy?
1	❌ No
2	❌ No
3	✅ Yes
4	✅ Yes
5	✅ Yes

Another child

Child B → Greed = 1

Now

Cookie Size	Child Happy?
1	✅ Yes
2	✅ Yes
5	✅ Yes
Important Rules
Rule 1

Each child gets only one cookie.

Not

Child → Cookie1 + Cookie2

❌ Not allowed.

Rule 2

Each cookie can be given to only one child.

If

Cookie size = 5

is given to Child A,

you cannot give the same cookie to Child B.

Objective

Your goal is

Make the maximum number of children happy.

Not

Give everyone the biggest cookie.
