A Greedy Pattern to Remember

This problem teaches a very common pattern:

Sort both arrays.
Start with the smallest items in each.
Maintain two pointers.
If they match, use both and move both.
Otherwise, discard the smaller one (move only that pointer).

You'll see this pattern again in many interview problems such as:

Assign Cookies
Matching jobs to workers
Boats to Save People
Advantage Shuffle
Some interval matching problems
